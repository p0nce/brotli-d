5. Tuning pass
--------------
[ ] Tune each @tuning constant, one by one, on the corpora, per level.
    All of them are open, including the ones already swept: re-test them
    after related changes. All change the output except TAGGED_MIN_LENGTH
    (speed only). Groups by the levels they affect; the order of groups and
    within groups is a guess of the expected gain, not a verdict. What was
    measured is noted as data.

    Levels 10-11: optimal parsing
    - kLevelParams q10 / q11: optimalPasses (2 / 4), optimalStarts (1 / 5),
      optimalMaxLength (150 / 325), optimalKeepBest; OPTIMAL_FIRST_TRIED_PASS
      (1). On dice the passes oscillate (321-322.7 KB over 10 passes), they
      don't converge.
    - LITERAL_CONTEXT_WEIGHT / LITERAL_ORDER0_WEIGHT (1 / 1): the parser
      keeps more literals than the reference's. Order-0 only (0 / 1): dice
      -0.24% at window 24 but +0.14% at window 18.
    - MISSING_SYMBOL_EXTRA_BITS (2): cost of a symbol absent from the
      previous pass. The reference's value, never measured here.
    - INITIAL_COMMAND_COST_OFFSET (11), INITIAL_DISTANCE_COST_OFFSET (20):
      costs of the first pass.
    - MAX_TREE_SEARCH_DEPTH (64), MAX_TREE_COMP_LENGTH (128),
      TREE_BUCKET_BITS (17), MAX_TREE_WINDOW (4 MB, 8 bytes of memory per
      byte), SHORT_MATCH_SEARCH_DISTANCE (64): which matches the parser sees.
    - OPTIMAL_MATCH_STARTS (2), START_MAX_COST_DIFF (0),
      LONG_COPY_QUICK_STEP (16384): limits of the shortest path search.
    - SEGMENT_CHUNK (16 KB), SEGMENT_PENALTY (510), SEGMENT_MAX_LENGTH
      (2 MB): swept in 718d6a2 on full inputs, chunk 8 / 16 / 32 KB, penalty
      380 / 510 / 765 (flat), max length 512 KB-4 MB (2 MB = 4 MB).
    - OMIT_FIRST_MIN_LENGTH (5): 4 / 5 / 6 / 8 were chaotic, +-0.1% per
      file; 5 best summed over text, C, 5 KB. 4 lost 0.04-0.08% on text and
      C at q10.

    Levels 5-11: block splitter
    - kSplitParams, per category, fast (5-9) / best (10-11): maxHistograms
      (16 / 100 literals, 50 others), symbolsPerHistogram, stride, minLength,
      switchCost, iterations (2 / 10), clusterBlocks. Retune since 47fb668:
      block clustering may now pay for more initial histograms or cheaper
      switches. clusterBlocks in the fast splitter: -0.2% to -0.3% for 30-57%
      of the speed (47fb668).
    - ABSENT_SYMBOL_EXTRA_BITS (2): cost of a symbol absent from a
      histogram. The reference's value, never measured here.
    - SPLIT_WARMUP (2000), SPLIT_WARMUP_START (77), SPLIT_WARMUP_SLOPE (7):
      cheaper switches at the start of a meta-block.
    - SPLIT_REFINE_FACTOR (2), SPLIT_REFINE_MIN (100): random samples that
      refine the initial histograms.

    Levels 4-11: cost estimates and prefix codes
    - SIMPLE_CODE_EXTRA_BITS (16), COMPLEX_CODE_BITS (17),
      COMPLEX_CODE_BITS_PER_DEPTH (0): estimated sizes of prefix codes in
      populationCost, used by every clustering, split, context mode and
      distance parameter decision. Simple codes are their exact sizes for
      8-bit symbols (12/20/28/37) + SIMPLE_CODE_EXTRA_BITS. History: the 4
      simple constants rose together over 3 rounds of +-2 (to 17/25/33/41),
      then were replaced by the offset, swept 5-32: 16 gives 100 B -2.9%,
      1 KB -0.13% vs 17/25/33/41; above 16 only 100 B gains (32: -3.7%)
      and 5 KB+ lose up to +0.14%. In all, from 12/20/28/37/18/2: 100 B
      -6.8% to -8.8% (text -6.9%, C -8.2%), 1 KB -0.15% to -0.74%, larger
      inputs within +-0.25%. Exact sizes per alphabet (4 + NSYM x alphabet
      bits) without offset: +0.44% vs 15/23/31/39.
      Open: an offset depending on the meta-block length (large for tiny
      inputs), after seeing which decisions it changes at 100 B. Split back
      into 4 constants (28/36/44/53) and tuned +-1 each: 29/37/43/54, -0.019%
      in all (1 KB +0.005%), noise: kept the offset.
      COMPLEX_CODE_BITS has a minimum inside -2..+2; PER_DEPTH 0 vs 1 is
      within noise.
    - RLE_STREAK_LIMIT (1240 / 256): optimizeHistogramForRle.
    - CLUSTER_BATCH (64): clusterMany with more than 256 histograms.

    Levels 0-9: greedy and lazy parsing
    - LITERAL_BYTE_SCORE (135), DISTANCE_BIT_PENALTY (30), MIN_SCORE_MARGIN
      (100), LAST_DISTANCE_BONUS (15), SHORT_CODE_PENALTY (39),
      LAZY_MATCH_MARGIN (175, levels 2-9): the match scores, which decide
      every match. The reference's values, never measured here.
    - kLevelParams q0-9: bucketBits, blockBits, numShortCodes, randomWindow,
      lazyMatching, hashLength, and the features enabled at each level.
    - DICTIONARY_THROTTLE_SHIFT (7, levels 2-11): when to stop looking up the
      static dictionary.
    - SKIP_NEAR_STEP (2), SKIP_NEAR_JUMP (8), SKIP_FAR_STEP (4),
      SKIP_FAR_JUMP (16), SKIP_FAR_FACTOR (4): skipping on incompressible
      data, speed and size on binary inputs.
    - TEXT_HASH_MIN_LENGTH (16 KB), TEXT_UTF8_PERCENT (75),
      TEXT_SAMPLE_LENGTH (64 KB): the 5-byte hash for text at levels 5-9
      (cb38c2b: text -1.2% to -2.3%, C -0.2% to -0.9%).
    - METABLOCK_LENGTH (1 MB). Meta-blocks chosen from the data at levels
      5-9, measured 2026-10-27: -0.16% to -0.25% mean size, -1.4% to -6.5%
      mean speed, level 9 D sources -44% speed; dropped.
    - TAGGED_MIN_LENGTH (1 MB): speed only (afa1608: images +15% to +43%).

    Levels 5-9: clustering
    - FULL_CLUSTERING_MAX_WORK (1 << 22): above it, clusterLiteralsByContext
      (8ec66c9: level 6 maps 2.9 -> 9.7 MB/s, size +0.13% at most). It is
      per meta-block: inputs cut into more meta-blocks pay the full
      clustering in each one.
