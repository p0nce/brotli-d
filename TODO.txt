Brotli encoder: remaining work
==============================

Rules
-----
- When the first measurement of a change looks marginal (small gain, or a
  speed cost that fails the per-level rate), show the numbers and ask
  whether it is worth pursuing, before profiling or tuning it.

Compression leftovers (small gains)
--------------------------------------
[ ] Small inputs vs the reference (100 chunks of 100 B / 1 KB / 5 KB of
    text and C, ours / reference, since SIMPLE_CODE_EXTRA_BITS): smaller
    everywhere (0.896-0.994) except C 100 B at q9 (1.027) and C 1 KB at q11
    (1.001). Before it: 100 B 1.00-1.13 at every level.
[ ] 16-bit PCM audio (more-test-files/vive-la-lune.wav, 437 KB): level 11
    is larger than the reference's (85.1% vs 84.7% of the input), the
    only new input where it loses; level 5 is much smaller (85.6% vs
    92.4%). Look at what q11 does with interleaved 16-bit samples
    (literal context mode, distance parameters, block splits).
[ ] pnggrad16rgb.png (4 KB): level 5 is 0.5% larger than the reference's.
[ ] Level 9 on 100 B inputs is 3.7% (text) to 4.9% (C) larger than level
    8 (0.997 vs 0.960, 1.027 vs 0.979 of the reference); 1 KB and 5 KB
    don't show it. q9 differs from q8 by tryAllContextModes,
    distanceParamSearch 2 and randomWindow 512: find which one, and why it
    costs on tiny inputs.
[ ] Dictionary transforms (prefixes, suffixes, uppercase) for levels 5-9.
[ ] Segmentation from better statistics than order-0 bytes (e.g. the
    literals and commands of a fast parse), or measured choices at level
    11: sizes still swing by +-0.5% with the exact cut positions.
