5. Tuning pass
--------------
[ ] Tune each @tuning constant, one by one, on the corpora, per level.
    All change the output except TAGGED_MIN_LENGTH (speed only). Most
    promising first:
    1. METABLOCK_LENGTH (1 << 20), levels 0-9. (OPTIMAL_METABLOCK_LENGTH
       done in 7d1b162: 384 KB, swept 64 KB-4 MB on full inputs; sizes jump
       by up to 0.9% between neighbor values, so data-chosen boundaries may
       gain more than any fixed length.)
    2. kSplitParams: switch costs, maxHistograms (100 / 50) and iterations
       of the best splitter. Retune since 47fb668: block clustering may now
       pay for more initial histograms or cheaper switches.
    3. LITERAL_CONTEXT_WEIGHT / LITERAL_ORDER0_WEIGHT: the parser keeps more
       literals than the reference's. Order-0 only (0 / 1): dice -0.24% at
       window 24 but +0.14% at window 18. Try 1 / 2, per input kind.
    4. kLevelParams q10-11: optimalPasses (4), optimalStarts (5),
       optimalMaxLength (325), and OPTIMAL_FIRST_TRIED_PASS. On dice the
       passes oscillate (321-322.7 KB over 10 passes), they don't converge.
    5. MISSING_SYMBOL_EXTRA_BITS (parser) and ABSENT_SYMBOL_EXTRA_BITS
       (splitter): the costs of absent symbols drive which symbols the
       parser and splitter avoid; 2 is the reference's, never measured here.
    6. RLE_STREAK_LIMIT, FULL_CLUSTERING_MAX_WORK, CLUSTER_BATCH: small,
       header and clustering effects.
